# Starward Siege

A 2D planetary space battle game designed to run smoothly at 60fps across desktop and mobile devices.

## Key Features

### Multi-Faction Planetary Warfare (2 Teams or 3 Teams)

- Choose between a **2-Team Planetary Duel** (Solari Vanguard vs. Crimson Dominion) or a **3-Team Galactic Clash** (Solari Vanguard vs. Crimson Dominion vs. Verdant Syndicate).
- Each team's home planet features a regenerating energy shield dome with directional impact ripples, dual orbiting planetary defense turrets, automatic hull repair
- ship-swapping when docked nearby, and AI escort squadrons.
- Contest the central Solar Relay to earn bonus points and a `+25%` fire-rate boost for your fleet.

### 4 Distinct Starship Classes & Abilities

- Viper Mk-IV (Interceptor): Twin rapid pulse blasters + Phase Dash & EMP Burst (blinks forward with invulnerability and clears nearby enemy bolts).
- Bastion-IX (Dreadnought): Explosive siege plasma shells + Aegis Deflector Nova (projects a damage-absorbing shield dome and blasts nearby foes).
- Nova Lance (Artillery): Hyper-velocity piercing railgun + Orbital Ion Beam (fires a continuous sweeping laser beam that sears ships and planetary armor).
- Valkyrie (Swarm Carrier): Homing micro-missiles + Deploy Drone Squadron (launches 4 autonomous attack drones).

### Tight Desktop & Touch Controls

- Desktop: `WASD` / `Arrow Keys` to fly, Mouse to aim (or keyboard-only steering), `Left Click` / `Space` to fire, `Right Click` / `Shift` / `E` for Special Ability, `1–4` to switch ships while docked/respawning, and `P` / `Esc` to pause.
- Mobile / Touch: Floating left-thumb virtual joystick + ergonomic right-thumb Fire (with soft target-assist) and radial-cooldown
